ST. MARY'S GENERAL HOSPITAL
HIPAA COMPLIANCE POLICY
Last Updated: March 2022

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes the procedures for protecting patient health information (PHI)
in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all workforce members who access, use, or disclose PHI.

3. ACCESS CONTROLS
Access to electronic PHI (ePHI) is restricted to authorized personnel only.
User accounts are assigned based on job function. Passwords must be at least
8 characters and changed every 90 days. Shared workstations must be locked
when unattended.

NOTE: Multi-factor authentication is recommended for remote access situations
where feasible, but is not currently required for all staff.

4. TRANSMISSION SECURITY
ePHI transmitted over external networks should use encryption where possible.
Internal network transmissions are protected by our firewall infrastructure.

5. AUDIT CONTROLS
System activity logs are maintained on all systems containing ePHI.
Logs are reviewed on a monthly basis by the IT department.

6. WORKFORCE TRAINING
All new employees receive HIPAA training during orientation. Annual refresher
training is encouraged but scheduling is at department manager discretion.

7. BUSINESS ASSOCIATES
The hospital maintains agreements with business associates who handle PHI.
A current list of business associates is maintained by the Compliance Office.
Business associate agreements are reviewed periodically.

8. BREACH NOTIFICATION
In the event of a suspected breach of PHI, the incident should be reported
to the Compliance Officer. The Compliance Officer will assess the breach and
notify affected individuals and HHS as required by law within 60 days.

9. RISK ANALYSIS
The hospital conducts risk assessments to identify threats to ePHI security.
Risk assessments are conducted when significant operational changes occur.

10. PHYSICAL SAFEGUARDS
Server rooms and areas containing PHI are restricted to authorized personnel.
Visitor access is logged.

END OF POLICY
